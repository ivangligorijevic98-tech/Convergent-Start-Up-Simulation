# Business Simulation Prototype

This repository contains a **high-fidelity Figma prototype** simulating a turn-based business dashboard. The main purpose of this prototype is to demonstrate how **player decisions in Pricing, Hiring, and Salary affect the Projected Impact panel** and overall quarter outcomes.

---

## 🏗 Prototype Overview

The prototype allows users to adjust key business decisions and see immediate feedback in the Projected Impact panel. The goal is to make cause-and-effect visible and testable, providing insights into user understanding and decision-making behavior.  

**Key Focus:**
- Visualizing **decision → impact flow**.
- High-fidelity mockup for testing, not a fully functional simulation.
- Clarity of feedback rather than full interactivity.

---

## ✨ What Was Scoped / Descoped

**Descoped / Deferred:**
- Competitor analysis was not included because there is no existing flow to compare; could be added after MVP.
- A full design system was not implemented; visuals prioritize clarity for testing.
- Full interactivity and draggable elements were not built due to time constraints.

**Assumptions:**
- Users understand the basics of adjusting Pricing, Hiring, and Salary.
- The prototype is meant for testing feedback from decisions, not simulating a real business engine.
- Later iterations could include interactivity, competitor insights, and a complete design system.
